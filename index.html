From PET waste back to PET.

Re-Du turns hard-to-recycle PET waste, from colored bottles and film to post-consumer carpet, into W2V™ Monomer (BHET), the molecular building block of PET and polyester. PET made from it, W2V™ PET, has the same chemistry as virgin PET.

01 — Why Re-Du

We start where mechanical recycling stops.

Mechanical recycling grinds and remelts clean, sorted bottles, and it does that job well.

Re-Du takes the colored, coated, adhesive-backed and mixed PET it can't use, including recyclers' own reject streams, and rebuilds it from the molecule up into PET with the same chemistry as virgin.

02 — How it works

Many kinds of waste in.

01

Waste in

Colored, coated, printed, adhesive-backed and mixed PET. Bottles go in with caps and labels still on.

02

The catalyst picks out the PET

A selective organocatalyst from Oak Ridge National Laboratory breaks only the PET down, below 200 °C at normal pressure, in commercially available equipment.

  • <200 °C
  • 1 atm
  • Licensed from ORNL
03

W2V™ Monomer comes out

Pure white BHET from any color of waste. Caps, labels, adhesives and coatings stay behind as separate solids.

  • BHET
  • Pure white
  • Solids filtered out

Back into W2V™ PET

The monomer feeds the polymerization lines PET producers already run. Same chemistry as virgin PET.

04 — Our second beachhead

Carpet. Only the PET reacts.

Most carpet fiber is PET, tangled up with polypropylene backing, adhesives, fillers and salts. Mechanical recycling can't untangle it, so almost all old carpet is landfilled. Our catalyst deconstructs only the PET into the same W2V™ Monomer, and leaves the rest behind to be recovered.

05 — Modular plants

We go to the waste.
The waste doesn't come to us.

Shipping low-value waste across the country costs more than it's worth. Re-Du's plants are modular and built near the waste: regional plants processing about 10,000 tonnes of PET waste a year, and smaller movable units planned for sites with less volume. Each plant makes W2V™ Monomer; PET producers turn it into PET.
